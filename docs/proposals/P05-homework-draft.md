# P05 — Homework (DRAFT functional specification)

**Status:** PROPOSAL for founder review. Not approved. Prepared for issue #1 (P05 follows P04).
**Based on:** `develop` @ `594d5af`: the inventory entry for P05 ("View subject-wise assignments and attachments", Must), P02 v0.1 (homework due card), T04 (teacher creates and edits homework) and A07 (homework oversight) in the inventory, DEC-006 (MVP first) and DEC-007 (modular and extensible).
**Items marked [Decision]** need founder input. References to PR #2, #3 and #4 are open proposal PRs, not yet merged.

## Product intent
P05 answers: **"What homework does my child have, and what do I need to open or print?"** It is a read-only view for parents. It is not a learning platform: no submission, grading or chat in the MVP.

## MVP scope (per DEC-006)
- **MVP:** upcoming homework by due date, subject filter, a recent-past list, item detail, and attachments (view or download).
- **Later (extensible):** completion tracking, submissions, teacher feedback, reminders, due dates on the calendar (P12), scheduled posting, voice notes, rich text, per-student homework.

## Business rules (proposed)
1. **Targeting:** homework is published to a class and section. Students in optional subjects or language groups need finer targeting, so only they see that homework **[Decision]**.
2. **Only published items are visible.** Drafts stay with the teacher.
3. **Due date semantics:** show "Due today", "Due tomorrow", or the date. With no completion tracking, there is no "pending" or "overdue" wording. After the due date, the item moves to Past. Whether a due date is mandatory, and whether it can fall on a holiday **[Decision]**.
4. **P02 homework card** shows a count of items due today and tomorrow plus the nearest subject, so it should say "due", not "pending". **[Decision: DEC-015 in PR #2]**
5. **Edits and deletions:** an edited item shows "Updated" with the time. Whether a change to the due date triggers a notification **[Decision]**. A deleted item disappears from parents' view, or shows "Cancelled" for a few days **[Decision]**.
6. **Attachments (proposed limits):** PDF and common image formats; up to 10 MB each and 5 per item; all files scanned before serving; access through private, expiring links; file size shown before download (many parents are on mobile data). Further types and limits **[Decision]**.
7. **History window:** Upcoming plus the last 30 days in Past, within the current academic year; older items are not shown in the MVP **[Decision]**.
8. **Subjects** come from the class configuration (A05). The teacher's name is shown with each item.
9. **Text content:** plain text in any language or script (for example Hindi), with long text wrapping and no formatting in the MVP. Whether links in text are clickable **[Decision]**.
10. **Notifications:** one notification per published item could be noisy, so options are per-post, a daily digest, or per-teacher limits **[Decision]**. Notifications respect quiet hours.
11. **Read state ("New")** is per parent account. It is not shown to teachers in the MVP.
12. **Out of scope for the MVP:** submission, grading, comments, per-student homework, and a learning-platform feature set.
13. **New admission:** a child admitted mid-year sees items within the history window for their class **[Decision]**.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A parent sees only homework published for the selected child's class and section (and subject groups).
2. Upcoming shows items due today or later, soonest first, with "Due today" and "Due tomorrow" wording.
3. After the due date, an item moves to Past and stays there for the history window.
4. The subject filter shows only that subject's items, and clearing it shows all.
5. An edited item shows "Updated", and a deleted item no longer appears (or appears as "Cancelled", per the decision).
6. Attachments open or download, show their size before download, and expire safely; links cannot be reused by someone else.
7. A file that fails to load shows a clear message and a retry.
8. P02's homework count equals the number of items due today and tomorrow for the same child.
9. "No homework due." appears when there is none, and the screen never looks broken.
10. A parent can never see homework or attachments for a child who is not linked to their account (verified by test).
11. Offline, the last loaded list and previously opened attachments are available, with a "last updated" banner.
12. The screen works with a screen reader and large fonts, in each v1 language, and with long titles and descriptions.
13. Switching child (P03) fully replaces the data.

## UX risks to design for
- **Photos of worksheets** are large and slow on weak connections: show size, compress thumbnails, and avoid auto-downloads.
- **"Pending" language** implies parents must act: use "due" until completion tracking exists.
- **Unnoticed changes:** an updated due date is easy to miss, so use a visible "Updated" marker.
- **Teacher over-posting** causes notification fatigue and muted apps: agree a notification policy early.
- **Several children and several subjects** can overwhelm: filter by subject and keep one child's context obvious.
- **Reading load:** long text in small type on small screens; test at large font sizes and with non-Latin scripts.
