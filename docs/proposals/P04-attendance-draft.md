# P04 — Attendance (DRAFT functional specification)

**Status:** PROPOSAL for founder review. Not approved. Prepared for issue #1 (P04 follows P03).
**Based on:** `develop` @ `594d5af`: the inventory entry for P04 ("View daily / monthly / term attendance", Must), P02 v0.1 (today's attendance card), the product definition (parent attendance view, teacher marking, admin reporting), the "Attendance Alert Flow" in the inventory, DEC-006 (MVP first) and DEC-007 (modular and extensible).
**Items marked [Decision]** need founder input. References to PR #2 and PR #3 are open proposal PRs, not yet merged.

## Product intent
P04 answers: **"How is my child doing on attendance, today and over time?"** It is a read-only view. It is also the screen a parent lands on after tapping an absence alert, so it must make today's status unmistakable and the history easy to scan.

## MVP scope (per DEC-006)
- **MVP:** today's status, a month calendar, period counts, and an attendance percentage.
- **Later (extensible):** term comparison charts, period- or subject-wise attendance, exports and downloads, minimum-attendance warnings, absence reasons.

## Business rules (proposed)
1. **One status vocabulary,** shared with P02: Not marked yet, Present, Absent, Late, Half day, On approved leave, Holiday, No school. Add "Not recorded" for a past school day where no attendance was ever submitted. **[Decision: DEC-008 in PR #2]**
2. **"Not marked yet" applies to today only.** It means the teacher has not submitted yet, and it never appears as Present or blank. After the school's cut-off time it stays "Not marked yet" to the parent and is flagged to the admin in A06 **[Decision: Q8 in PR #2]**.
3. **Past school days with no record** show "Not recorded", are excluded from the percentage, and are reported to the admin.
4. **Daily attendance only** for the MVP. Period- or subject-wise attendance is a later extension.
5. **Single source of truth:** the latest saved value is shown everywhere (P02, P04, notifications). If a teacher or admin corrects attendance after submission, parents see the corrected value. Whether a correction triggers a notification **[Decision]**, and whether a corrected day shows an "updated" marker **[Decision]**.
6. **Late and Half day** are set by the teacher in T03 **[Decision]**.
7. **Approved leave:** a day covered by an approved leave is displayed as "On approved leave" and counted as an excused absence. Precedence when the teacher also marks Absent **[Decision]**.
8. **Percentage:** proposed formula = (Present + Late + 0.5 × Half day) ÷ (school working days with a recorded status), rounded to one decimal. Holidays, no-school days, "Not recorded" and "Not marked yet" are excluded. Approved leave counts as absent in the percentage but is shown separately **[Decision]**.
9. **Working days** come from the school calendar (A05, A14): holidays, 5- or 6-day weeks, and half-day Saturdays are school-configured **[Decision]**.
10. **History scope:** the current academic year in the MVP; previous years are read-only or hidden **[Decision]**. A child admitted mid-year starts from the admission date. Withdrawn or transferred children follow the account-state rules in P01.
11. **Absence reasons or teacher notes** are not shown in the MVP **[Decision]**.
12. **Time zone:** all dates and times are in IST.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Today's status on P04 always equals the status on P02 and in the attendance notification for the same child and day.
2. Before the teacher submits, today shows "Not marked yet", never Present and never blank.
3. After submission, today's status updates and the "Updated at" time reflects it.
4. The month calendar shows one status per school day, and holidays and no-school days are visually distinct from absences.
5. Summary counts and the percentage match the calendar for the selected period, using the agreed formula.
6. A future date never shows a status.
7. Tapping a day shows its status and, if on approved leave, a link to that leave.
8. A correction made by the teacher or admin appears to the parent as the corrected value.
9. A parent can never see attendance for a child who is not linked to their account (verified by test).
10. Offline, the last loaded data is shown with a clear "last updated" banner.
11. Status is shown by icon and text as well as colour. The calendar works with a screen reader (each day announces date and status) and at large font sizes.
12. Switching child (P03) fully replaces the data; nothing from the previous child remains.

## UX risks to design for
- **Colour-only status** in a dense month grid: use icon plus text, and test for colour-blindness.
- **Percentage anxiety:** explain the formula in one line, avoid alarming colours except against a school-configured minimum **[Decision]**.
- **"Not marked yet" versus "Not recorded"** can confuse: use clear, different wording and a short help text.
- **Week start and numerals:** Monday or Sunday start, and localised month and day names per the languages chosen for v1 (Q7 in PR #2).
- **Small screens:** the month grid must stay tappable on low-end phones; consider a list view for accessibility.
