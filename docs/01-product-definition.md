# SchoolBridge — Product Definition

## Business Idea

SchoolBridge is a parent-engagement platform for Indian private schools that brings the most important parent-school interactions into one simple experience.

### Parent promise

**Everything important about your child, in one place.**

### School promise

A simple, reliable digital channel to keep parents informed and reduce repetitive administrative communication.

## Primary Users

### Parent
Uses the mobile app to view child-related information and submit requests.

### Teacher
Performs frequent class-level actions such as attendance, homework updates, notices, and leave review.

### School Administrator
Manages school structure, students, parents, teachers, classes, fees, communications, reports, and user access.

## Initial Scope

### Parent Experience
- OTP / secure login
- Child switcher for multiple children
- Home dashboard
- Attendance
- Homework
- Notices / announcements
- Fees and payment visibility
- Leave request and status
- Notifications
- Calendar / useful school information

### Teacher Experience
- My classes
- Mark attendance
- Post homework
- Publish class-level communication
- Review relevant leave requests
- Student list

### School Administration
- Dashboard
- Student / parent management
- Teacher management
- Class / section management
- Imports / exports
- Fee setup and fee visibility
- Attendance reporting
- Notices
- Leave administration
- Roles / access
- School settings

## Out of Initial Scope

The following remain future candidates and are intentionally excluded from the initial product baseline:

- Full ERP replacement
- Payroll / HR
- Library management
- Admissions platform
- RFID hardware
- Live school-bus GPS tracking
- AI assistant
- Advanced report-card / examination engine

These may be revisited after the initial product validates usage and customer demand.
